    You stay in control

    Before you join.

    Confirm the Wi-Fi name and sign-in instructions with staff. A familiar name or advertised encryption does not verify who runs it.

    How to join on iPhone
    1. Open your iPhone’s Settings, then Wi-Fi.
    2. Choose the exact network the venue confirmed you can use.
    3. Follow the venue’s password or sign-in instructions. Stop if something does not match what you were told.

    This companion cannot select or join a network for you. When unsure, use your own mobile connection or hotspot.

    Keep this companion on your Home Screen

    When this page is served over HTTPS, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it once online so the app shell can be available offline.

    The app shell can be cached. Imported reports are never cached.

    Your hosting provider may ask you to sign in before opening this companion. Reports are read on your device.
